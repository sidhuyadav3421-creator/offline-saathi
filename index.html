Emergency pocket guides

Local incident notes

Encryption protects stored notes at rest, but this is an unaudited prototype. Use a strong passphrase and do not store sensitive identifying details.

Encrypted local vault

Set a passphrase or unlock your vault. Losing the passphrase means losing access to notes.

Older unencrypted v0.1 notes detected

Migration copies older notes into the encrypted vault after you unlock it. Originals stay unencrypted in your old browser database until you explicitly delete them. Only migrate notes you wish to retain.

Organic Maps

Download the Delhi/NCR map inside Organic Maps while online. GPS and downloaded map navigation can then work without mobile data. This toolkit does not contain street-map tiles.

organicmaps.app ↗
    Offline maps cannot verify temporary road, station, police or access restrictions. Never assume a suggested route is open or safe.

    Messaging without internet: what works

    Briar — Android only

    After setup and contact exchange, nearby Android phones can communicate by Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi. There is no iPhone version and no automatic citywide relay.

    briarproject.org ↗

    Bridgefy — Android / iPhone

    Bluetooth messaging and claimed relay capability; first-time activation may need internet. Privacy and reliability limitations make it unsuitable for sensitive information.

    bridgefy.me ↗

    LocalSend — photos and files

    Transfers files between devices on the same local Wi-Fi network, even if that network has no internet. It does not work as a Bluetooth mesh.

    localsend.org ↗
    This website itself does not send Bluetooth messages or create a mesh network. Those functions require separate native apps, compatible devices, permissions and testing.

    Safety and privacy

    How offline installation works

    After one successful HTTPS visit, the service worker caches this website's interface and guides. Reopen the installed app without internet to test. Browsers may clear cached data, so offline access is not guaranteed forever.

    Open source

    MIT licensed demonstration. Audit and security review required before widespread or high-risk use. Version 0.1 — not an emergency dispatch service.
